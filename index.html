Road data

This page cannot call map servers itself. Load OpenStreetMap road data once and it fills in speed, lanes, sidewalks, crossings and nearby hazards for every point. Check what it fills in against what you see.

A few points: load road data (3 taps)
  1. Tap 1 Open road data. A new tab opens with text.
  2. In that tab press and hold the text, choose Select all, then Copy.
  3. Come back here and tap 3 Paste road data.

Many points: upload map data files

Download the fetch script, run it on a computer with Python (python3 fetch_map_data.py points.csv), then upload the mapdata.json it makes. You can upload several files.
